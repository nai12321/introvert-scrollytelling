ABOUT THE DATA

A note on interpretation

The source data are survey responses from separate online samples. The behavior dataset has no demographic fields and 388 exact duplicate rows. The supplemental datasets should not be pooled with respondent-level records.

These findings describe patterns in recorded responses. They do not diagnose personality, establish causality, or represent the population.
