For my project, I plan to create a scrollytelling-style webpage focused on the visualizing and explaining any findings from introvert data I’ve found on the web. This is because I wanted to use my DS skills for something that may be helpful for others to understand themselves better. The dataset I’ve found contains 2900 responses to a college survey, with columns being: Time_spent_Alone, Stage_fear, Social_event_attendance, Going_outside, Drained_after_socializing, Friends_circle_size, Post_frequency, and Personality. If this is not enough data, I plan to supplement it with further datasets, though finding non-synthetic datasets may prove difficult. The final visualization will be hosted on my GitHub page, and will be linked.
